# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, harassment-free community for everyone.

Be respectful, assume good intent, discuss technical evidence rather than people, and make room for different levels of experience. Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, doxxing, and publishing another person's private data are not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove abusive content and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository maintainers or GitHub's reporting tools. Do not use public issues for sensitive reports.
